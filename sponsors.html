Why the Lions

Real visibility, not a banner nobody sees.

RSEQ restricts commercial branding on the game jersey itself — so sponsor placement lives on the warmups and warmup pants instead. That's program-owned gear our players are required to wear, at every practice and every game, with no RSEQ conflict. On top of that, your brand shows up in the video content the program already produces — reels, highlights, game recaps — and in front of the players, coaches, and families who follow the team.
