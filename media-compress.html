Lions Media Toolkit — Compress

COMPRESS CLIPS

Shrink videos right on your iPad, iPhone or computer — nothing to install, nothing uploads. Pick clips, pick a size, hit Compress, then save them back to Photos. Back to the main toolkit.

1. Pick Clips

This browser can't record video in the page. On iPad/iPhone, update iPadOS/iOS and use Safari; on a computer, use Chrome or Edge.

2. Size

How much it shrinks depends on what you start with: 4K clips (roughly 170–400 MB per minute) easily drop 5–15×, but a clip that's already 1080p "High Efficiency" (about 60 MB per minute) only has room for 2–4× before it looks blocky. "Max" works out the right setting per clip. Output is H.264 MP4, which opens everywhere — Photos, Drive, Windows and Edge.

3. Compress

No clips yet — tap Choose Videos.

It plays each clip through once to re-record it smaller, so a 1-minute clip takes about 1 minute. Keep this page open and on screen until it finishes — switching apps or locking the iPad pauses it.
